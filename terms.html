Services and prices

Through this website we supply and install gas boilers. Prices shown in our quote tool are fixed prices for a standard like-for-like installation and include the items listed with each boiler. If extra work is needed that was not described when you booked, we will explain it and agree the price with you before carrying it out. Prices may change without notice, but the price confirmed in your order is the price you pay for the work described.

Quotes and bookings

A quote is based on the information you give us and may change if the job turns out to be different from what was described. Some jobs need to be inspected before a final price can be confirmed. Please tell us about anything that may affect the work, such as access problems.

Booking and paying online

You answer questions about your home in the quote tool, choose a boiler, any extras and your preferred installation date, and send us your details. You are then taken to a secure payment page run by Stripe. Your booking is made when you have sent your details and completed payment. Your preferred date is a request; we confirm the date and arrival time with you by phone or email. Please give accurate answers and details, including the installation address and postcode, as our fixed price depends on them.

Prices and payment

Online payments are taken through Stripe. We do not see or store your full card details. You can pay a deposit or the full price when you book.

Boiler installation: deposit, cancellation and balance

A £500 deposit is taken online to secure a boiler installation booked through our quote tool. The remaining balance must be paid in full in cleared funds before installation work begins.

Because each boiler installation is individually assessed, equipment and materials may be ordered specifically for the agreed installation. If you cancel after Southwark Heating has incurred costs specifically for your installation, we may retain or deduct an amount reasonably reflecting costs actually incurred as a result of the cancellation. Where possible, we will take reasonable steps to minimise any loss, including seeking refunds from suppliers where available. Any remaining amount will be refunded where applicable. Nothing in these terms affects your statutory consumer rights.

When you pay the deposit you are asked to confirm that you request us to begin arranging or providing your installation before the end of any applicable 14-day cancellation period. We record that confirmation with the date, time and your order reference. This is subject to applicable consumer law.

If an agreed installation appointment is missed because the customer is unavailable, access to the property is not provided, or the installation cannot proceed due to circumstances within the customer’s control, Southwark Heating may retain an amount from the deposit that reasonably reflects the direct loss incurred, up to £225. This is not an automatic penalty.

Access and your responsibilities

Please make sure we have safe access to the property and the appliances and areas where work is needed (for gas safety work, this includes the boiler and all relevant gas appliances), and that someone aged 18 or over is present if required. We may refuse or stop work if conditions are unsafe.

Gas safety

All gas work is carried out by a Gas Safe registered engineer. If an appliance or installation is unsafe, we may need to disconnect or turn it off, and we will explain why. If you smell gas, call the National Gas Emergency Service on 0800 111 999.
