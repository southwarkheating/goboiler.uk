Quick answers

How fast can I get a price?

Straight away. Answer the questions in the quote tool and you see fixed prices for the boilers that suit your home on screen. No home visit and no waiting for a call back for most homes.

How soon can the boiler be fitted?

Any day from tomorrow, and up to 60 days out. Once you have paid, we ring you to lock in the day and give you an arrival time.

Is the price really fixed?

Yes, for a standard like-for-like installation. If something extra is needed on the day, we explain it and agree the price with you before we do it.

Who fits the boiler?

Gas Safe registered engineers from Southwark Heating (registration 985690), based in SE16. goboiler.uk is one of Southwark Heating’s websites.
